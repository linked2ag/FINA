Download

FINA Book is a cash book for one year: twelve months, three kinds of money, one file. Your figures stay on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and no server — the app writes into a JSON file that you choose and that you can read yourself.

You do not have to download anything to try it: FINA Book runs in the browser, with the same files. The app is for when you want it in your dock, without a tab and without the internet.

Would you rather have it installed properly, with a start menu entry? Windows installer — same program, and it can update itself in place later on.

The first start takes a few clicks

FINA Book is not signed yet. A signature costs 99 € a year at Apple and about 120 $ at Microsoft, and FINA Book does not cost anything. So your system does not know who wrote the program and says so — clearly, and in the case of macOS rather harshly. The program is fine; nobody has vouched for it.

macOS:

  1. Open the DMG and drag FINA Book into Applications.
  2. Double-click FINA Book. macOS refuses and offers only “Move to Bin”. Say no.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll right down.
  4. There it says “FINA Book was blocked”. Click “Open Anyway”.
  5. Enter your password, then confirm once more.

Windows: “Windows protected your PC” → “More info” → “Run anyway”. Two clicks. Some virus scanners are wary of unsigned programs as a matter of course.

Every following start is a normal double-click. And you can check for yourself what you downloaded: the checksums are below.

Requirements — macOS 12 or newer · Windows 10 (64-bit) or newer
Your data — one JSON file wherever you keep it. Replacing the app does not touch it.
Checksums — SHA256SUMS.txt with the release
Updates — the app tells you when a new version is out and sends you here. You can switch that off in the settings; it is the only time FINA Book talks to the network.

Der erste Start kostet ein paar Klicks

Dein System weiß deshalb nicht, wer das Programm geschrieben hat, und sagt das auch — deutlich, unter macOS sogar unfreundlich. Mit dem Programm ist alles in Ordnung; es hat nur niemand dafür gebürgt.

macOS:

  1. Die DMG öffnen und FINA Book nach „Programme“ ziehen.
  2. FINA Book doppelklicken. macOS weigert sich und bietet nur „In den Papierkorb legen“ an. Nicht darauf klicken.
  3. Systemeinstellungen → Datenschutz & Sicherheit öffnen und ganz nach unten scrollen.
  4. Dort steht „FINA Book wurde blockiert“. Auf „Trotzdem öffnen“ klicken.
  5. Passwort eingeben, danach noch einmal bestätigen.

Windows: „Der Computer wurde durch Windows geschützt“ → „Weitere Informationen“ → „Trotzdem ausführen“. Zwei Klicks. Manche Virenscanner sind bei unsignierten Programmen grundsätzlich misstrauisch.

Jeder weitere Start ist ein gewöhnlicher Doppelklick. Und du kannst selbst nachsehen, was du geladen hast: die Prüfsummen stehen unten.
